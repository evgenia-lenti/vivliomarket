The Vivliomarket platform — Η πλατφόρμα

Built for the way
books change hands

Vivliomarket is a marketplace for second-hand books in Greece. Every copy carries a graded condition, every payment waits in escrow until the book arrives, and every parcel has a label ready at the locker down the road — so a stranger's shelf is as safe to buy from as a shop.

Explore the platform

Listings
& search

Scan a barcode and the title, author, edition and cover fill themselves in. Buyers find the copy by ISBN, by course code, or by browsing the shelf it belongs to.

Escrow
payments

The buyer's money is held from checkout until delivery is confirmed, then paid out to the seller. Nobody sends a book on trust, and nobody pays into thin air.

Shipping
& pickup

Print a label for a Box Now or ACS locker, or hand the book over in your own city. Tracking updates land in both inboxes without anyone chasing them.

Condition
& trust

A six-point condition scale backed by photos, ratings earned from completed orders, and a moderator on every reported listing.
